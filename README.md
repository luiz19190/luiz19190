## About me

I'm Luiz, a data analyst based in Porto, Portugal. I completed the **TripleTen Data Analyst** programme in 2026 with ten public end-to-end projects, from exploratory analysis to A/B tests, cohorts, a churn model and Tableau dashboards. Every project answers a real business question and ends in a recommendation someone can act on.

- Final project: why only **2.01%** of 58,139 Olist first-time buyers came back within 180 days, and why a late first delivery turns **75.63%** of ratings into 1 to 3 (against 18.17% on time).
- Technical Diploma in Systems Development (Brazil) and a study exchange in Ontario, Canada. Fluent in Portuguese and English.
- Before data: warehouse operations at Lidl Portugal and semi-professional basketball. Both taught me to read the game and help the team decide.
- Next on my list: Excel certification and Power BI.
